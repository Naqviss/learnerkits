This project preserves both notices; the converted anatomical asset may therefore be treated under the stricter attribution/share-alike terms if required.

The browser-ready `bodyparts-heart.glb` is an adapted package of the heart component meshes FJ2417–FJ2439, excluding IDs that are not part of the heart. Geometry was centered, reoriented, assigned educational chamber materials, and exported to glTF Binary. No anatomical geometry was generated by AI.
